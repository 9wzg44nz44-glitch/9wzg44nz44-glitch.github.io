Prad at 5 A is ~749 W with Z₀ ≈ 376.73 Ω — a large current for a stub, which is why the toys look optimistic. Real 23 cm amateur power is far lower; see the Lab legal box.

Mix-and-match antennas (from the original page)

Purity factor ≈ fraction of power in the irrotational / longitudinal-potential mode. Hively bifilar aims near 1; a conventional dipole is much lower. Plasma detectors primarily sense time-varying A; bifilar coils convert longitudinal E into gradient current. Path loss in that simulator was a generalized 1/rn (default n=2) plus optional medium dB/km — again a cartoon.
