Symbols. SLW: scalar-longitudinal wave, the untested wave of Hively's extended electrodynamics (HYP). SW: scalar wave, the E = 0 special case (HYP). TEM: ordinary radio (FACT). σ: electrical conductivity of the medium (S/m). α: attenuation. The field falls by a factor e every 1/α metres; 1 Np/m = 8.686 dB/m. dB: decibels of power.

Shielding. The receive sphere and its detector sit inside their own Faraday cage (the RadioScreen tent), which strips ordinary radio (TEM) noise (FACT for TEM; see the cage control below). That an SLW passes through the cage wall is a HYPothesis.

Why Joule loss is the default, the seawater numbers, and the Erratum toggle (sources)

Hub rule (25 Sep 2026): include Hively's Joule-loss (Ohmic) term for SLW in conductive media. Hively & Loebl, Phys. Essays 32, 112 (2019), Eq. (44), has the Joule term J·E in the energy balance, with Ohm's law J = σE (HYP). Experiment C's field dictionary lists SLW as “Eddy / skin: no; Ohmic (Joule): yes (J=σE∥)”, lists SW as “no” for both, and says: “Do not say ‘SLW has no losses.’ Say: no eddy / no classical skin barrier; SLW still has linear Ohmic loss; SW has neither.” Its Ohmic section adds that longitudinal E “still drives J=σE and local Joule density σE∥². Pure SW (E=0) has no σE² term.”

How much, in seawater. We use Experiment G's code unchanged (experiment-g.html, hub PR #56, not yet published, so the link may 404 for now; seawater ε′, ε″, σ from Meissner & Wentz 2004, FACT). The Joule-literal attenuation is αJ = σH|Z|/2, with σH = ε0ε″ω and |Z| from Hively's Eq. (37). That is our reading of Eq. (44) (ASSUMPTION), not an equation Hively prints. TEM-equal uses the textbook plane-wave αTEM (FACT). Air conducts almost nothing, so both are ≈ 0 in air (ASSUMPTION σair ≈ 0). Copper and steel walls have no model yet (UNKNOWN), so they add 0.

This page's αohm multiplies power density S (S = Sgeom·e−αohmL). Experiment G's α is a field coefficient, so αohm = 2α (a unit conversion, not new physics). Path losses use the page's locked 4.343·αohm·L.

Erratum α = 0: a non-default toggle (HYP). Hively & Loebl's 2022 Erratum argues that the Joule term cancels. What it says, in context (Phys. Essays 35, 320, 2022, doi:10.4006/0836-1398-35.3.320):

“This Erratum corrects errors in Appendix A of our paper for derivation of the no-skin-effect constraint. The scalar longitudinal wave (SLW) has only irrotational fields and no magnetic field from Eq. (27), B = 0 … Substituting the expressions for E and C from Eqs. (18) and (20) into Eq. (A.5) gives the longitudinal component of the A-wave equation: … (A6). Physically, Eq. (A6) means that the SLW is not subject to resistive loss in conductive media. The same no-loss result occurs from the energy balance equation, Eq. (44), by substituting for (ρ/ε) from Eq. (22) and by substituting for JL from Eq. (21) with B = 0; then cancelling terms. Consequently, the SLW is unconstrained by the skin effect and power loss in resistive media.”

With the toggle on, no medium absorbs anything (α = 0). Choose it in “SLW loss in conductive media” below.

S = S_geom * exp(−α_ohm L_eff)   # α_ohm = power coefficient; Erratum toggle / SW → α=0
