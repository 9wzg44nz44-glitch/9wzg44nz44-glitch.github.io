Papers on the desk

Local PDFs, copied verbatim — not rewritten. Genre labels matter: the first block is the 1.3 GHz / Faraday-cage hardware literature. Adjacent LENR, anecdote, NASA survey, laser-plasma, and company-newsletter items follow so they are not silently mixed into the lab protocol.

Inclusion is not endorsement. NASA contractor reports are not NASA certification of over-unity. Company newsletters are marketing. AHE/phonon papers are condensed-matter / LENR-adjacent, not bifilar manuals.

Primary sources for the 1.3 GHz lab

Primary · McMaster 1.3 GHz

Nikolova, N.K. & Zimmerman, R.K. (Nov 2007). Detection of the Time-dependent Electromagnetic Potential at 1.3 GHz. CEM-R-46.

Computational Electromagnetics Laboratory, McMaster University. Wireless link designed so field vectors are ideally zero while the 4-vector potential is not; longitudinally polarized magnetic vector potential; detection with biased low-temperature plasma. Authors conclude RF 4-potential is measurable and receiver RF power is converted from local bias, not radiated TX power.

Download PDF

Primary · amateur RF

Zimmerman, R.K. (2011). Transmission and Reception of Longitudinally-Polarized Momentum Waves.

QEX July/August 2011, pp. 31+. Author NP4B / VE3RKZ. Five-page Epson scan. TEM vs vector-potential comparison and a 1296 MHz circular-waveguide transmitter: 7-inch galvanized stovepipe, TM01, λcut ≈ 23.23 cm, 69 cm guide + 69 cm longitudinal probe, 5.8 cm choke collar, Type N + 3 dB pad, U-shaped fluorescent plasma detector.

Download PDF

Primary · L-band range

Zimmerman, R.K., Jr. (2011). Macroscopic Aharonov–Bohm Effect at L-band Microwave Frequencies.

Modern Physics Letters B 25(9), 649–662. TM01 circular waveguide (7-inch, 3 m) 1280–1380 MHz; folded mercury-vapor plasma detector at 200 mA; free-space range claimed 1.5 m out to 25 m+. Argues Lorenz gauge is the physically correct gauge for propagating A waves.

Download PDF

Primary · plasma antenna

Zimmerman, R.K., Jr. (2013). Reception of longitudinal vector potential radiation with a plasma antenna.

Journal of Applied Physics 114, 044907. NASA Goddard / Sigma Space. Time-harmonic vector-potential reception at 1.3 GHz using a plasma antenna; interaction energy e[φ − v·A] with plasma drift velocity collinear with A.

Download PDF

Works, G. & Works, S. (2012). Confirmation Measurements of Vector Potential Waves.

QEX July/August 2012. George Works (KJ6VW) and Shelley Works (KG4SRS). 1296.1 MHz antenna-range confirmation of Nikolova–Zimmerman-style vector-potential observations; authors also discuss a conventional electromagnetic interpretation.

Download PDF

Primary · patent

Hively, L.M. (2016). Systems, apparatuses, and methods for generating and/or utilizing scalar-longitudinal waves. US 9,306,527 B1.

Gradient Dynamics LLC. Skirt-balun monopole and series-opposing bifilar coil; 8 GHz Faraday-cage / copper-pipe+hemisphere tests this hub scales to 1.3 GHz HAM.

Download PDF

Primary · IARD 2020 slides

Hively, L.M. & Land, M. (2020). Extended Electrodynamics and SHP Theory. IARD 2020 slides.

72-page PowerPoint. “Evidence for Scalar-Longitudinal Photons.” Experiments credited to Andy Loebl; equipment access Ali Fathy (UTK); partial funding Gradient Dynamics LLC. Hardware numbers used in the 1.3 GHz lab protocol.

Download PDF

Primary · EED theory

Hively, L.M. & Land, M. (2021). Extended electrodynamics and SHP theory.

J. Phys.: Conf. Ser. 1956 012011. Argues incompleteness in CED/QED; EED as resolution; Stueckelberg–Horwitz–Piron theory equivalent to EED.

Adjacent / not lab hardware

NASA contractor report

Alexander, D.S. (April 2005). Advanced Energetics for Aeronautical Applications: Volume II. NASA/CR-2005-213749.

MSE Technology Applications, Inc., Butte, Montana. Institutional survey of advanced energetics (including Tesla-vs-Hertz, Valone, Puthoff US 5,845,220, Meyl in §3). Treat as a map of claims, not as NASA endorsement of those claims.

Download PDF

AHE / phonon · not SLW hardware

Hubler, G.K. (2023). Microscopic Insights into the Anomalous Heat Effect that Unify Disparate Experimental Results.

J. Condensed Matter Nucl. Sci. 38, 78–88. Phonon-resonance model of the anomalous heat effect in Pd/Ni systems (electrolysis, pressure, ultrasound, glow discharge). Adjacent to LENR literature. Keep it labeled as AHE/phonon, not as a Faraday-cage or bifilar protocol.

Download PDF

Mainstream plasma physics

Yue, D.N. et al. (2021). Dynamics of Moving Electron Vortices and Magnetic Ring in Laser Plasma Interaction.

Laser-plasma PIC study (Shanghai Jiao Tong / collaborators). Legitimate vortex / longitudinal-structure reference in high-intensity laser plasmas. Useful as a reminder that “vortex” and “longitudinal” are ordinary words in plasma physics — not proof of Hively SLW in copper boxes.

Download PDF

Educational-historical

Zhvirblis, V.E. (1995). The Bagel Game. Chemistry of Life. Tr. Bob W. Greenyer.

Toroidal “bagel” coil / electromagnetic-phantom anecdote from a Moscow basement. Nested spiral → torus, low-frequency generator, a sensor signal that did not behave as the author first expected. Historical-literary, not a lab protocol.
