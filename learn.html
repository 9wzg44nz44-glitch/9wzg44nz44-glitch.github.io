Briefing: three vocabularies for “not TEM”

Nikolova–Zimmerman, Hively, and Meyl do not say the same thing. They start from different missing terms, build different hardware, and should be cited separately. This page is a map, not a verdict.

SLW / longitudinal-A / potential-vortex claims are active research and fringe relative to textbook CED. Treat every number below as literature or illustration unless you measure it.

Classical baseline (what they all say is incomplete)

Textbook Maxwell–Heaviside in SI:

∇·D = ρ    ∇·B = 0    ∇×E = −∂B/∂t    ∇×H = J + ∂D/∂t

Potentials that automatically satisfy the homogeneous pair:

B = ∇×A    E = −∇Φ − ∂A/∂t

The Lorenz leftover, usually set to zero as a gauge condition rather than as a Maxwell equation:

C ≡ ∇·A + (1/c²) ∂Φ/∂t    (Lorenz gauge: C = 0)

Free-space communications waves are TEM: E ⊥ k, H ⊥ k, S = E×H ∥ k. A metal wall attenuates TEM over a skin depth

δ = 1 / √(π f μ σ)    copper σ ≈ 5.8×10⁷ S/m, μ = μ₀

At 1.300 GHz, δ_Cu ≈ 1.833 µm. At 8 GHz, δ_Cu ≈ 0.739 µm. Faraday cages work for TEM when walls are many skin depths and holes ≪ λ. Whether a longitudinal / potential channel ignores that attenuation is the experimental question — not a theorem of this webpage.

Three programs

N–Z

Physical A, TM01 launch

Engineers treat A as auxiliary; this line treats longitudinal A as measurable (macroscopic Aharonov–Bohm). Tx: monopole in a circular waveguide below TEM cutoff (TM01-like). Rx: DC-biased plasma / fluorescent U-tube aligned with k. Signature: reversing bias reverses RF phase. Detected RF power is argued to come from the local bias supply, not TEM Poynting flux.

Am = μ₀ Im / (2π k₀ r)
IRMS = R · Am/√2
R ≈ 1.86×10⁵–2.64×10⁵ m/H

Hively

Keep C; B≈0 SLW

Does not throw out E and B. Promotes C to a dynamical field. SLW: B≈0, C≠0, longitudinal E along ∇C. Tx: linear monopole + λ/4 skirt balun forcing irrotational (gradient-driven) current JL=∇κ. Rx: bifilar pancake, adjacent turns opposing, net B≈0, face-on to EL. Patent claim: unconstrained by classical skin effect.

Prad = I²/(4π) · √(μ/ε)
= I² Z / (4π)   (US 9,306,527 Eq. 15)

Meyl

Potential vortex τ₂

Starts from Faraday and adds a relaxation term B/τ₂. “Scalar wave” here is a longitudinal E-structure whose speed is not locked at c in his reading. Hardware: paired Tesla pancakes, spheres, LED-in-a-box kits. Bruhn (JSE) argued the scalar-wave condition plus source-free Maxwell leaves no oscillating wave. Over-unity and v>c are claims, not defaults in this hub.

How the detectors are supposed to work

Plasma U-tube (N–Z)

Core sentence from the original explainer: free electrons in a low-pressure plasma have high mobility; an incident longitudinal AL couples to kinetic momentum and modulates drift velocity along the tube, producing RF current on the bias circuit.

pmech = mv = pcan − qA

In metal, induced RF velocity is argued to be millimetres per second; in a fluorescent plasma, tens of metres per second. Orient the long axis ∥ k. Reverse DC bias → reverse RF phase. Because B=∇×AL=0 for a pure longitudinal wave, a metal loop has no classical induction to work with.

Zimmerman QEX 2011; J. Appl. Phys. 114, 044907 (2013); plasma responsivity cited ≈ 4.2×10⁵ A per Wb/m (2011 §5) or 1.86–2.64×10⁵ m/H (CEM-R-46 modeled).

Bifilar pancake (Hively)

The coil is geometrically matched to irrotational (gradient-driven) fields. Adjacent turns carry opposing current → net B≈0 and near-zero inductance. What remains is a gradient response: EL or −∂AL/∂t drives a coherent irrotational current to the terminals.

JL ≈ σ EL    EL = −∇Φ − ∂AL/∂t

Do not parallel the two wires. Series-opposing (outer of A to inner of B) is the non-inductive connection. Face-on maximizes projected area (~78.5 cm² for a 10 cm palm coil in the SNR toys).

US 9,306,527 B1; Hively & Land, J. Phys.: Conf. Ser. 1956 012011 (2021).

Zimmerman 2011 hardware — 1296 MHz circular waveguide

First-class amateur-radio source (scan in the Library): Robert K. Zimmerman, NP4B / VE3RKZ, “Transmission and Reception of Longitudinally-Polarized Momentum Waves,” QEX July/August 2011, p. 31+. TEM vs vector-potential comparison and a 1296 MHz TM01 transmitter:

This is the natural N–Z-style transmitter to sit alongside the Hively RG-405 stub + skirt in the 1.3 GHz lab. It is still an experimental interpretation: a TM01 launcher plus a plasma detector does not, by itself, prove a new free-space channel.
