Theory upgrade · Rentzber (2026)

Natan Rentzber, Scalar-Longitudinal Radiation in Extended Electrodynamics with Multipole Theory and a Compensated Source Model, arXiv:2608.19405 (2026). Conditional EED predictions — does not claim charge nonconservation in nature. Key takeaways for experiment design:

  1. Source: the scalar channel is sourced only by Λ = ∂ρ/∂t + ∇·J (continuity anomaly). Bound polarization / magnetization sources do not source C.
  2. Far-zone SLW: EL, B=0, C co-propagating; cos²θ dipole pattern vs Maxwell’s sin²θ.
  3. Detector corollary (critical design constraint): a conserved test current extracts no cycle-averaged energy from a pure incident SLW under the adopted field-only balance. Nuclear detectors therefore need either (a) coupling to residual EL / potentials, (b) an anomalous detector Λd, or (c) a non-adopted-current observable — phase, EFG, or electron density at the nucleus (EC). Ordinary ohmic pickup alone is the wrong primary meter for a pure SLW claim.
  4. Lineage (cite lightly as adjacent theory): van Vlaenderen & Waser; Minotti & Modanese detector papers.

Why nuclei (the publishable hook)

Nuclei carry charge multipoles, magnetic moments, and (for quadrupole nuclei) couple to electric field gradients (EFG). If C is a real scalar EM degree of freedom with energy density ∼ C²/(2μ), the open question is whether it couples to nuclear charge/current multipoles beyond ordinary E/B. That coupling — bounded, falsifiable, honestly framed — is what would make a paper. A null bound is publishable too. Rentzber’s energy-null for conserved currents pushes the menu toward EC density, EFG/NQR, PAC, Mössbauer, and phase / sideband observables rather than “just count betas near the launcher.”

Channel A · charge / continuity

Back-action of C onto nuclear charge

EED’s source for C is (∂ρ/∂t + ∇·J). Nuclear transitions rearrange charge; a back-action of C onto nuclear charge density would appear as a phase or rate shift. Effective sketches (to be bounded experimentally):

δHC ∼ gC · Qnuc · C   or   ∫ ρnuc C dV

with gC a dimensionless coupling. For quadrupole nuclei, prefer

δH ∼ (κ/2) Qij ∂i∂j χC

where χC is a potential whose Laplacian relates to C. Keep honest: the microscopic operator is not settled — the experiment bounds effective couplings, it does not invent them.

Channel B · EFG bridge (Hubler) · library · collaboration

PAC / EFG as a nuclear readout

Hubler (2023), J. Condensed Matter Nucl. Sci. 38, 78–88, uses perturbed angular correlation (PAC; 181Hf→181Ta) to measure EFG at the nucleus in PdH/D. That paper is AHE / phonon · LENR-adjacent — not a bifilar or Faraday-cage protocol (see Library). EFG is an ordinary electric-field-gradient observable at nuclear sites. An SLW-class longitudinal E (or a C-correlated gradient) that modulates the local EFG would be a legitimate, measurable nuclear readout — even if AHE/phonon claims stay adjacent and unlabeled as proven on this hub.

Channel C · spin / DNP · adjacent, not C proof

Ventura–Sokol / Falcon / Alzofon as a parallel program

Mainstream DNP/DNO (Jeffries, Dynamic Nuclear Orientation, 1963): microwave-driven transfer of electron polarization to nuclei near EPR — an accepted NMR-enhancement method. That is ordinary condensed-matter physics.

Falcon Space / Mark Sokol (APEC coverage by Tim Ventura; public Falcon materials) test Alzofon’s hypothesis that nuclear orientation might affect apparent mass / inertia. Framing on this hub:

  • Public sources only (e.g. Falcon DNO–mass PDF; Alzofon 1981 AIAA; Ventura/APEC interviews as journalism).
  • S/X-band DNP inside a Faraday cage is a nuclear-spin control channel — useful as “nuclei as sensors/actuators.”
  • Alzofon–Sokol inertia/gravity claims are speculative hypotheses, not accepted physics, and are not Hively C proof.

Mapping: DNP is a parallel “nuclei as sensors/actuators” program this hub refuses to exclude; it is not evidence that dynamical C couples to nuclei. Keep as parallel adjacent — not primary.

Channel D · decay rates · contested vs accepted (reframe)

Contested solar / Fischbach claims vs accepted EC chemistry

Contested (do not elevate): environmental / solar-modulation claims of decay-rate variation (Fischbach, Jenkins, and others). Metrology reviews (Pommé et al.; Norman/Cooper-style nulls) attribute many reported signals to systematics. Frame carefully: somewhat discussed / contested, not “well established.” Generic β Geiger counting alone is a weak primary channel.

Accepted (elevate as the serious decay family): electron-capture (EC) rates that track electron density at the nucleus — especially 7Be chemical-host shifts (~0.1–1%). See Channel E. Proposal for any decay side channel near an SLW-class source: temperature / humidity / background locks — the same caution already on SW vs SLW. This is not evidence already in hand on this bench.

Channel F · 14N NQR · tabletop EFG sensor

Nuclear quadrupole resonance without CERN PAC

14N NQR (MHz) reads the EFG at nitrogen sites without a PAC collaboration. Homemade spectrometers exist (e.g. Hiblot / Canet-class fully homemade 14N NQR; modern SoC-FPGA compact NQR). Sensitivity is the hard part (cryo, averaging, sample mass).

Experiment: NQR sample (e.g. NaNO2 / pharma 14N) co-located with an SLW bifilar/sleeve; look for line shift / broadening vs RF-off / TEM stub / Faraday wrap. Direct EFG bridge complementary to Hubler PAC (Channel B).

Channel G · scalar Aharonov–Bohm / time-varying Φ · mainstream QM · SW-leaning

Energy / phase shifts from potentials in (ideally) field-free regions

Scalar AB: phase or energy from a time-varying scalar potential in regions where E and B are (ideally) zero. Neutron interferometry: Allman et al., Phys. Rev. Lett. 68, 2409 (1992); Phys. Rev. A 48, 1799 (1993). Atom interferometry and related work. Energy-level sidebands from time-varying V(t) with E=0: Chiao et al., Phys. Rev. A 107, 042209 (2023). PDF now local in the Library (arXiv:2212.03437v2 preprint matching the published PRA).

Mapping: closer to Hively SW (E=B=0, potentials/C) than to SLW. Experiment path: Faraday-cage / shielded volume with modulated Φ (or C-proxy) and NMR / atomic clock / Ramsey probe for sidebands — distinct from cage-attenuation SLW tests.

Label carefully: scalar AB is accepted QM for known Φ; whether Hively C equals that Φ coupling is the open EED claim. Not an SW detector unless the triad is logged.

Channel H · Mössbauer / nuclear resonance γ · collaboration

High-Q nuclear γ readout

RF / magnetic fields create Mössbauer sidebands (classic Heiman / Pfeiffer / Walker work). Modern coherent X-ray control of 57Fe nuclear excitons: Heeg et al., Nature 590, 401–404 (2021); doi:10.1038/s41586-021-03276-x — educational pointer to γ spectroscopy as a high-Q nuclear sensor near an SLW source if collaboration access exists. Garage rank: low without a Mössbauer lab.

Channel I · distinguish EED’s C from beyond-SM scalar bosons

HCI / g-factor exclusion-plot culture — not the same object

Precision HCI / bound-electron g-factor / “nuclide shift” papers constrain hypothetical light scalar mediators coupled to electrons/nucleons (e.g. arXiv:2508.00685; arXiv:2608.23703, 2025–2026 class). Note: EED’s C is an EM 4-potential divergence field, not the same object as a Yukawa scalar boson — but the experimental culture of bounding new scalar couplings to nuclei (exclusion plots) is the publishable template for reporting |gC| or |δλ/λ| bounds from Channels E–G.

Atomic adjacent · Hively patent hint · not nuclear proof

mS→nS / 1S→2S electronic transitions

US 9,306,527 mentions facilitating mS→nS (1S→2S) atomic transitions. Keep as an atomic (electronic) adjacent channel — optional optical / atomic probe near SLW — not nuclear proof.

Experiment menu (falsifiable sketch)

Educational design. Part 97 / licensed RF where applicable. Success = a published bound (or a clean null) on |δλ/λ|, NQR/EFG shift, or sideband amplitude vs a C-proxy amplitude. Rank for garage/HAM lab vs collaboration:

  1. 7Be (or other EC) counting with locks — best “accepted physics → EED bound” path (Channel E).
  2. 14N NQR shift hunt near 1296 / 433 MHz SLW launcher (Channel F).
  3. NMR / Ramsey / clock sideband hunt in a shielded Φ/C volume — SW-leaning (Channel G).
  4. PAC/EFG or Mössbauer if collaboration (Channels B, H).
  5. Keep DNP / Alzofon as parallel adjacent, not primary (Channel C).
  • Primary launcher: SLW-class — 1296 MHz sleeve + bifilar or 433 MHz balls; keep the two feeds electrically separate (A/B benches separate; same rule as the lab pages).
  • Controls (unchanged): RF off, TEM stub, Faraday wrap, distance scan, T / humidity / background locks.
  • Rentzber constraint: prefer observables that are not pure conserved-current energy extraction (EC density, EFG/NQR, phase/sidebands).
  • Explicit: this is not an SW detector unless the triad is logged (SW vs SLW). PMT / decay side channels are optional SLW effects, not SW proof.
