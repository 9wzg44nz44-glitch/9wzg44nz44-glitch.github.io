A 1296 cage B 433 balls+rods Kit

Shared field kit · end-state · not built

Two cubes, mesh Faraday, both antennas as cargo

This is the packing / hull plan for taking Experiment A (1296 MHz sleeve-stub + pancake) and Experiment B (433.59 MHz balls + rods) into the field. It is not built yet. The kit lists both antennas as cargo — it does not mix RF feeds. Pancake protocol stays on setup.html. 433 parts stay on monstein.html / sphere.html. Index summary: index.html#kit.

Plastic Pelican = hydrostatic shell. RadioScreen mesh = Faraday. Do not drill the hull (kills IP67 and the warranty). Pelican 1200 on the setup shopping list is the small TinySA/coil travel case, not this hull.

TX: one cubical watertight case

One cube. Foam holds the 1296 sleeve-stub sled and the 433 TX sphere in separate wells — cargo, not a combined feed. Wrap the inner pack in RadioScreen. The plastic Pelican is the hydrostatic shell; the mesh is the Faraday. Use a flat side of the cube to aim the stub axis.

  • 1296 path still includes the 23 cm BPF inside the Faraday wrap (setup.html#bpf).
  • 433 TX sphere is MW Fig. 1 style: coax into the ball, outer as shield, no balun (Experiment B).
  • No mixed RF tee between the sleeve-stub and the ball.

RX: second case, usually lid open

Second cube. In use the lid is usually open, with a mesh tent, so two TinySA screens stay readable (1296 detector and 433 detector). This case is not submerged.

Two tents from the RadioScreen piece already on setup.html#mesh. Roll, don’t crease, for travel.

Harmonics (why sequential is first)

The two licensed tones sit on each other’s harmonic grid:

  • 1296 / 3 = 432 MHz, which is 1.59 MHz from 433.59 MHz.
  • 433.59 × 3 = 1300.77 MHz vs 1296 MHz.

A TinySA Ultra generator at 1296 MHz is already in the square-wave / mixer region. Simultaneous operation therefore needs:

  1. 23 cm BPF on the 1296 TX (already in the A chain: ZX75BP-1307-S+ or DEM-BPF).
  2. 70 cm BPF on the 433 TX (not the pancake kit — log the SKU when purchased).
  3. Separate foam wells; both antennas still inside the mesh wrap.

Sequential first. Simultaneous is a later logged article, not the first wet test.

Ballast — empty cube floats; do not drill

Pelican 0340 buoyancy is 220 lb. An empty cube floats. Do not drill the hull — that kills IP67 and the warranty.

  • Primary seawater path: NRS 1″ HD cam straps through the four fold-down handles, plus shot bags.
  • No VHB pad-eyes as the primary seawater path.

Depth rating

IP67 = 1 m / 30 min, not continuous seawater. First article: pool / shallow FL. Later seawater is a logged separate step, not implied by buying the cube.
