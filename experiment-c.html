Experiment C — Faraday-caged SLW/SW source with atomic 1S–2S optical-UV readout

A third bench, separate from the 1296 MHz cage (A) and the 433 MHz balls+rods (B). Goal: ask whether a Faraday-caged scalar-longitudinal / scalar-wave source can move population on a dipole-forbidden S–S interval (hydrogen 1S–2S, or a documented clock-atom S–S), with ordinary TEM fields vetoed at the atom, while dual solar-blind PMTs count later TEM UV at 243 nm and 121.6 nm. Theory and patent context: Hively US 9,306,527, Hively/Reed EED, Navy SLW antenna work. Atomic optical readout is a predicted channel. TX and detector banks below list stub / sphere / bifilar / NZ-style plasma and PMT + side transducers; the SNR companion includes SLW Ohmic path loss.

Do not treat 8 GHz as a 10.2 eV photon. An RF SLW launcher (GHz class) is not a single quantum that bridges the 1S–2S gap. See § Frequency plan. The PMT does not hear the C-wave; it hears later TEM UV after the atom returns by an allowed channel.

Exact claim under test

Claim (v1): A Faraday-caged SLW/SW source transfers population on the 1S–2S interval of hydrogen (or a documented clock-atom S–S), or modulates that interval in a lock-in sense, while companion TEM-null sensors show E⊥ and B below a pre-registered TEM leakage budget at the atom location.

  • Pass is not “we saw some UV.” Pass requires modulation transfer + TEM veto + quench discriminator (below).
  • 243 nm alone is the known two-photon diagnostic — not proof of SLW.
  • 121.6 nm with quench on is ordinary Lyman-α from 2S→2P mixing. The interesting channel is 121.6 nm that tracks the SLW/SW modulator with quench off (and TEM budget held).

Frequency plan (explicit)

1S–2S gap ≈ 10.2 eV two-photon diagnostic: 243 nm ≈ 1.23×10¹⁵ Hz = 1.23 PHz full gap as one quantum / Lyman-α λ: 121.6 nm ≈ 2.47×10¹⁵ Hz = 2.47 PHz RF cage lineage (this hub / Hively tests): ~1.3 GHz (Expt A) or ~8×10⁹ Hz class 8 GHz / 1.23 PHz ≈ 6.5×10⁻⁶ → not a direct pump

v1 architecture (chosen): RF SLW/SW as modulator / gate, not as the 10.2 eV photon.

  1. Optical clock / calibration arm (TEM, known physics). A 243 nm two-photon source (or a commercial clock-laser stack for a chosen S–S atom) establishes the line, calibrates the PMTs, and provides a source-dark baseline. This arm is ordinary CED/QED.
  2. RF SLW/SW arm (contested). Faraday-caged skirt-balun monopole and/or bifilar coil at a licensed / lab-safe RF (reuse Experiment A 1296 MHz cage practice, or an 8 GHz-class launcher if licensed gear exists). Amplitude-modulate the RF. Inside the shield at the atom: TEM-null dipole + loop (or TinySA) prove E⊥, B below budget.
  3. Coupling hypothesis under test. C(t) and/or E∥(t) either (a) move population on the S–S interval, or (b) Stark-like / phase-modulate the interval so the optical count-rate envelope locks to the RF AM. The PMT still only sees TEM UV after the atom returns by an allowed channel.
  4. Not in v1. Claiming the RF quantum itself is 10.2 eV; claiming a garage nano-antenna >1 THz is ready; treating Navy US 12,525,711 as a PMT document (it is RF launch/receive).

If a future revision uses a real VUV / optical-scale C-coupled source as the pump, rewrite this section — do not silently re-label GHz RF as PHz.

Incoming SW/SLW is not the photon the PMT sees. Navy SLW antenna patents do not put a PMT on the launcher — the PMT belongs only on the atomic optical readout.

How C or SLW becomes a PMT count

  1. C(t) and/or E∥(t) interact with the atom (scalar monopole term qC/εμ and/or longitudinal E).
  2. Population moves on the forbidden S–S interval (prediction).
  3. Atom returns by an allowed TEM channel: two-photon 2S→1S at 243 nm, or quench 2S→2P→1S Lyman-α 121.6 nm.
  4. PMT + bandpass counts that ordinary UV. Lock-in: UV count-rate envelope should track AM of C² or |E∥|² (and CE cross term on SLW).

Why 121.6 nm was called “hypothesized”: the line is real (Lyman-α). Ordinary origin = 2S mixed to 2P then 2P→1S (proves n=2 only). Direct same-parity single-quantum 2S→1S is the EED claim — only interesting if 2S–2P mixing is vetoed and the rate tracks the modulator.

Parts list (v1 class — not a shopping cart yet)

Optical / vacuum

  • Hydrogen cell or beam, or a clock atom with a documented S–S interval if H is too hard for v1.
  • MgF₂ or LiF window on the 121.6 nm path; vacuum or N₂/Ar purge (air kills Lyman-α in millimeters). 243 nm can run in air with a suitable window.
  • Optional: ion collector for classic 2S photoionization readout.

Detectors

  • Solar-blind PMT class: CsI photocathode preferred for 121.6 nm; Cs-Te (or similar) for 243 nm. Solar-blind ≠ substitute for a bandpass.
  • Bandpass filter 1: ~243 nm. Bandpass filter 2: ~121.6 nm.
  • HV supply for PMT (~kV class); counting / lock-in electronics.

RF / SLW side

  • Faraday cage / RadioScreen tents (reuse Experiment A practice on setup.html).
  • Skirt-balun monopole and/or counter-wound bifilar coil (Hively / Navy geometry). Licensed frequency — do not invent an unlicensed 8 GHz radiator.
  • TEM-null: small dipole + loop inside the shield at the atom, or TinySA Ultra as a residual E/B monitor (same kit language as A/B).
  • AM modulator + reference to DAQ.

Optical clock arm

  • 243 nm two-photon stack (or commercial clock laser for the chosen atom) for source-dark line scan and PMT calibration.

Model numbers and SKUs will be filled after a buy pass; this list is the discriminator-complete class list from the 9 Sep 2026 brief.

Safety

  • UV-C / VUV: 243 nm and especially 121.6 nm — eye and skin hazard; no direct viewing; enclosed beam paths; interlocks where practical. Ozone generation near VUV — ventilation.
  • PMT HV: ~kV supplies; treat as lethal; bleed resistors; never probe dynodes live.
  • Hydrogen: flammable; leak check; no ignition sources; follow compressed-gas SOP. Prefer sealed cell over open flow for v1 if possible.
  • RF: licensed amateur / ISM only; Faraday wrap for experimenters as well as the claim; no magnetron protocol (same hub rule as A).
  • Educational disclaimer: contested physics; not for medical, free-energy, or operational communications claims.

SLW Ohmic / propagation loss (do not forget)

SLW is not lossless. No eddy / no classical skin pile-up (B=0), but longitudinal E still drives J=σE and local Joule density σE∥². Pure SW (E=0) has no σE² term. Do not say “SLW has no losses.”
  • Local Ohmic density (CED + EED agree on the form): p_Joule = J·E = σ E∥².
  • Hively US 9,306,527: with J=σE, the SLW E equation admits a transient E ∝ exp(−ε t / σ) (patent EQN. 12 family) and table item that E, C, and J exchange energy in conductive media (EQNs 11–17). Free-space radiated power cartoon is isotropic P_OUT (patent EQN. 15 / JPCS Eq. 22) falling as 1/r² — that is geometric spreading, not Ohmic.
  • JPCS 2021: SLW impedance Z uses JL=σEL and loss tangent tan δε = ε″/ε′ (their Eq. 21); still no eddy skin term.
  • SNR implication: path through air ≈ geometric 1/r²; path through seawater / metal / lossy dielectric must include an extra Ohmic attenuation for SLW. For a pure SW trial set Ohmic α→0 (theoretical) and demand E∥ monitors stay null.

v1 run outline

  1. Commission optical arm alone: 243 nm line, PMT dark counts, bandpass check.
  2. Commission cage + TEM-null budget with RF on, atom cell empty or blocked.
  3. Load atom; RF off; repeat 243 nm baseline.
  4. RF AM on; quench off; lock-in 243 and 121.6 channels vs AM.
  5. Quench on/off map at fixed RF AM.
  6. Nulls: RF off, detuned RF, intentional TEM leak (open mesh flap) as negative controls.
  7. Write results against the pass/fail table; do not upgrade language past what the discriminators allow.
