Simulation Lab catalogue

Every Simulation Lab simulator, analysis and report that has a live page, grouped by topic. Each card links to its page and shows its FACT / HYP / mixed badge and the date it last changed (git log).

FACT mostly cited, textbook or measured content HYP mostly hypothesis content MIXED both, with claims labelled on the page
Publishing rule (from 2026-09-27). Every new simulation or analysis ships with a live GitHub Pages twin, linked from this catalogue, before the task counts as done. Pages and Wolfram Cloud may go live at different times (relaxed rule, 2026-09-25); a page ahead of its Cloud twin says "Cloud twin pending sync (vX)". Only Experiments A–G get Cloud twins. Outbound drafts and private notes are never published. Under the SLW hypothesis no skin-depth, permeability, ferromagnetic or cage-SE argument is used; TEM passages are tagged transverse-EM reasoning, NOT applicable under the SLW hypothesis.

Card colours: green top = report page added 2026-09-28; blue top = separate repo; red top = finished work that is owed a page (held or needs Dan's sign-off); copper = in progress in a parallel PR.
