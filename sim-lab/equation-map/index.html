Equation source and meaning map for the SLW simulators

For Dan's learning. Every equation the hub's SLW simulators use, with the equation as printed in its source, the exact source (paper, equation, page), what each symbol means in plain English with units, which hub pages use it, and whether it is FACT, HYP, ASSUMPTION or a known typo.

MIXED ← Simulation Lab catalogue · Three readings of SLW loss · Joule-loss positions · Corrections log · P_OUT audit

How to read this page. FACT = printed in the named source (equation, page or column given). HYP = Hively's SLW hypothesis or our inference. ASSUMPTION = a modelling choice made on a hub page. TYPO as printed = a known printing error, with the correction. DERIVED = algebra on printed equations. Where a symbol's definition is not in a source we have, the page says "not defined in the source we have" rather than guessing. Equations are taken only from the source files on the box (pdftotext of the papers and the notes recorded in the hub), not from memory. Under the SLW hypothesis no skin-depth, permeability or ferromagnetic argument is used; the allowed loss terms are Hively's Joule term σE∥² (2019 Eq. 44, as corrected by the 2019 erratum) or none (2022 Erratum). TEM reasoning is tagged transverse-EM reasoning, NOT applicable under the SLW hypothesis. The 2019 erratum PDF is private: its equations are cited, the PDF is neither embedded nor linked. Nobody has been contacted.

Extended electrodynamics (EED) equations 18–22

FACT HYP

E = −∇Φ − ∂A/∂t (18)   B = ∇×A (19)   C = ∇·A + (1/c²)∂Φ/∂t (20)
∇×B − (1/c²)∂E/∂t − ∇C = μJ (21)   ∇·E + ∂C/∂t = ρ/ε (22)

Source. Hively & Loebl, Phys. Essays 32, 112 (2019): Eqs. (18)–(22), p. 114 (charge density is printed "q" in the paper). Equation (20) is the definition of the scalar field C. The paper says "From Eq. (20), C has the same dimensions as B = μH" (p. 116).

Energy balance, Eq. (44) (and patent EQN. 14)

Erratum 2019: the electric energy density is εE²/2. Patent EQN. 14 prints εE²/2 and "J·E − ρC/(εμ) = 0".

Source. J·E is in Hively & Loebl 2019 Eq. (44), p. 117. The substitution J = σE is the patent's wording (col. 6, "which can be rewritten using J = σE") and Hively & Loebl App. A (A4). Charge relaxation E = E₀ exp(−σt/ε), τ = ε/σ

FACT (author-confirmed) TYPO as printed (printed forms)

Correct: E = E₀ exp(−σ t / ε), τ = ε/σ   |   printed in 2019 (A5) and in the patent, col. 5: E = E₀ exp(−ε t / σ) (ε and σ swapped)

Source. Hively & Loebl 2019, App. A, Eq. (A5), p. 122 (typo as printed). US 9,306,527 B1, col. 5, before EQN. 12 (typo as printed; the patent writes ∈ for ε). See corrections. The 2022 Erratum drops the transient.

Glossary

SLW (scalar-longitudinal wave)
In Hively's extended electrodynamics (EED), a wave made of the longitudinal electric field EL and the scalar field C, with no magnetic field (B = 0). A hypothesis, not established physics.
EED / CED
Extended electrodynamics (Hively's theory, with the C field) versus classical electrodynamics (Maxwell's equations with the Lorenz gauge, C = 0).
C field
The scalar field defined by Eq. (20): C = ∇·A + (1/c²)∂Φ/∂t. Same dimensions as B. Zero in classical theory, dynamical in EED.
Irrotational / longitudinal
A field with zero curl (∇×F = 0). The SLW's EL and JL are curl-free.
Joule term (J·E = σE∥²)
Power per volume that the electric field delivers to conduction current. The allowed SLW loss term on this hub.
E∥
The electric field component parallel to the current or wire. Each hub page defines it locally; Hively does not define it for the SLW.
σ, ε, τ
Conductivity (S/m), permittivity (F/m) and relaxation time τ = ε/σ (s).
Loss tangent tanδ
ε″/ε′, the ratio of the lossy to the lossless part of the permittivity.
Np/m and dB/m
Attenuation units. 1 Np = 8.686 dB for a field amplitude. A field falling as exp(−αr) loses power as exp(−2αr).
P_OUT
Hively's "power output" from a monopole, Eq. 38/B5. On the hub: a power density in W/m² at range r.
Null / image source
A distance at which the direct and the ground-reflected waves cancel. M&W treat the ground as producing an image of the source.
RCS (radar cross-section)
The equivalent area that would give the same echo if it re-radiated equally in all directions.
FACT / HYP / ASSUMPTION
FACT: taken from a source with equation and page. HYP: Hively's SLW hypothesis or our inference. ASSUMPTION: a modelling choice on a hub page.
TEM
Transverse electromagnetic wave (ordinary radio). Any TEM reasoning on the hub is tagged "transverse-EM reasoning, NOT applicable under the SLW hypothesis".
