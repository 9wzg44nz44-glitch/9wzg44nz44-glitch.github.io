Simulation Lab audit · P_OUT · Hively & Loebl 2019 erratum · 2026-09-29

P_OUT per the Hively & Loebl erratum: audit of every simulator

The erratum (Phys. Essays 32(3), 417) corrects Eq. 38 and B5 to P_OUT = I² r̂ / [2 (4πr)²] · √(μ/ε) and Eq. 44 to εE²/2. This page records what each hub simulator used, whether it matches, and the numeric change at the preset distances.

MIXED ← Simulation Lab catalogue

Scope. This page audits every hub simulator, calculator and Mathematica/Python source for how P_OUT (radiated power), the medium impedance √(μ/ε), I² versus I, and the factors 2 and (4πr)² are computed. It is an analysis page: no Wolfram Cloud twin is planned. Citations: Hively & Loebl 2019 (Phys. Essays 32, 112) and the Erratum, Phys. Essays 32(3), 417 (2019). Labels: FACT = paper- or erratum-sourced, HYP = our inference or the SLW hypothesis itself, ASSUMPTION = a choice made here. Under the SLW hypothesis no skin-depth, permeability or ferromagnetic argument is used; the only loss term allowed is Hively’s Joule term σE∥² (2019 Eq. 44) or none (erratum α = 0 toggle).

1. What the erratum says (FACT)

The erratum is one page: “Erratum: Classical and extended electrodynamics [Phys. Essays 32, 112 (2019)]”, Lee M. Hively and Andrew S. Loebl, published online 18 Sep 2019, DOI 10.4006/0836-1398-32.3.417, p. 417. The whole correction, quoted:

“There were typographical errors in the recently published paper. The correct form of Eq. (38) is
    P_OUT = I² r̂ / [2 (4πr)²] · √(μ/ε)                                   (38).”

“The correct form of Eq. (44) is
    ∂/∂t ( B²/2μ + C²/2μ + εE²/2 ) + ∇·( E×B/μ + CE/μ ) + J·E = ρC/(εμ)   (44).”

“The correction to Eq. (38) results from correction of Eq. (B5)
    P_OUT = I² r̂ / [2 (4πr)²] · √(μ/ε)                                   (B5).”

P_OUT and r̂ are set in bold in the erratum. The relayed form (P_OUT = I² r̂ / (2 (4πr)²) · √(μ/ε)) matches the erratum exactly; there is no difference to flag.

2. What the erratum does not say, and what the 2019 paper supplies

  • The erratum does not state the original printed form, and it does not define r̂ or I. (FACT: it is silent.)
  • Original Eq. 38 and B5 (FACT, read from the 2019 paper, pp. 116 and 123): P_OUT = I² r̂ / (4πr)² · √(μ/ε), with no factor 2. The erratum therefore inserts the factor 2 in the denominator, i.e. a ½.
  • Original Eq. 44 (FACT, same pages): the electric energy density was printed as εE², where the erratum has εE²/2. Every other term is the same. The 2019 text on pp. 116–117 already writes “4πr²(εE²/2)”, so the erratum confirms that the missing /2 was typographic.
  • r̂ (FACT, 2019 p. 116, after Eq. 37): “the unit vector in the radial direction”. So P_OUT is a vector along +r̂ with the magnitude I²/[2(4πr)²]·√(μ/ε).
  • I (FACT, 2019 Appendix B): “the current”, a phasor amplitude with e^{jωt} time dependence. The paper does not say peak or rms. Appendix B takes the outward flow from the time-averaged radial ⟨CE/μ⟩ of Eq. 44 and calls it isotropic, falling as r⁻². Hively & Land 2021 (J. Phys.: Conf. Ser., Eq. 22) already prints the corrected 2(4πr)² form and calls it the time-averaged SLW power.
  • √(μ/ε) (FACT): the impedance of the medium. In free space it equals η = Z₀ ≈ 376.73 Ω. It is η, not 1/η.
  • Not corrected by the erratum (FACT): US 9,306,527 Eq. 15, P_OUT = (I²/4π)√(μ/ε), in watts. Reed & Hively 2020 (Symmetry, Eq. 23) still prints the old form without the 2.

3. Units check of the erratum form as printed

I² [A²] · √(μ/ε) [Ω] / (4πr)² [m²]  =  W / m²

Integrated over a sphere of area 4πr²:   ∮ P_OUT · dA = I² √(μ/ε) / (8π)   [W]
Patent Eq. 15 (older, in watts):             I² √(μ/ε) / (4π)   [W]

So the erratum expression is dimensionally consistent as a power density (W/m², directed along r̂), even though the paper calls it “power output”. There is no dimensional error to repair. Two consequences follow. (a) The hub calls the total-watts quantity P_rad or P_OUT, and it derives S = P_rad/(4πr²); this is the same object as the erratum’s P_OUT once P_rad = I²Z₀/(8π). (b) The erratum total is half the total in patent Eq. 15. Both statements are algebra on FACT equations (HYP-free), but which of the two the paper means by I (peak or rms) is not settled by the erratum.

ASSUMPTION used everywhere below: I is the peak amplitude of the drive current. This is what the hub code already used (I_pk = √2·I_rms from the 50 Ω identity), and the ½ then plays the role of the time average. If Dan reads I as rms, the ½ would be double-counted and the change would need revisiting.

4. What the simulations used before (from code and repo notes)

  • Code: every I-driven page computed P_rad = I_pk² Z0 / (4π) (watts), then S = P_rad / (4π r²), which is S = I_pk² Z₀ / (4πr)². That is numerically the 2019 Eq. 38/B5 as first printed (no ½), and it is patent Eq. 15 spread over a sphere. Z₀ was used as η = 376.73 Ω (correct; never 1/η).
  • Repo notes: expt-d/REPORT.md records B5 as “P_OUT = [I² r̂/(4πr)²]√(μ/ε)” (line 29) and flags the missing ½ as ±3 dB (line 164). PHYSICS_LOCK.md, physics-constants.json and sleeve-balun-snr/RESEARCH.md record the patent Eq. 15 chain. Earlier notes (the internal Joule-term comparison note, JOULE_LOSS_POSITIONS) listed the 2019 erratum as unread; it has now been read.
  • Experiment D used the same as-printed form (Z0·I_pk²/(4πr)²), and in its modulation engine the factor 2 in 2·Z0·S_I/(4πr)² came from I_pk² = 2·I_rms². Under the erratum that 2 cancels against the ½.

9. Update 2026-09-30: units decision, peak confirmed, older repos and Experiment D fixed

  • ASSUMPTION I is the peak amplitude: confirmed by Dan Britton, 2026-09-30. The paper and the erratum still do not say; the label stays ASSUMPTION.
  • FACT Units decision (labels only, no number changed). The paper quantity is P_OUT (W/m²) = I_pk² Z₀ / [2 (4π r)²] at range r (Hively & Loebl 2019 Eq. 38/B5 as corrected by the 2019 erratum). The total watts, 4π r² · P_OUT = I_pk² Z₀ / (8π), is shown only as a clearly labelled DERIVED / ASSUMPTION quantity (spherical integral, isotropic). Hub pages that called the total-watts quantity P_rad or P_OUT now label it P_total (derived) and use P_OUT for the W/m² value; JavaScript identifiers such as Prad are unchanged.
  • Documented difference: patent US 9,306,527 Eq. 15, total I² Z₀/(4π) W, is twice the erratum-integrated total I² Z₀/(8π). The erratum does not correct the patent; patent quotes stay as quotes.
  • Experiment D (separate repo) fixed and live: Expt D PR #7, physics expt-d-detect-v0.3.1, squash ef74fb6. Experiment D PR #6 (Experiment G pointer) stays open and unmerged; main was merged into its branch.
