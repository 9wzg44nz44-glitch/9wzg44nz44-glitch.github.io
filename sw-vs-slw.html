Why this bench is adequate for SLW and not for SW

Scope: Hively EED split between scalar-longitudinal wave (SLW) and scalar wave (SW). This page does not claim SW has been detected here. Simulator pages stay separate.

Public summary (do not strengthen): This repository’s hardware and the published Hively/Loebl cage tests are SLW-class: they launch and receive a gradient-driven channel with suppressed B and a non-zero longitudinal E. That is sufficient to study SLW attenuation, Faraday penetration, and seawater loss. It is not sufficient to claim detection of Hively’s SW (E=B=0, C≠0). No public, replicated, modulated SW-only link through seawater and steel exists. Hull-transparent signalling remains a theoretical SW advantage, not a result of this bench.
Core lock-in: this setup couples to (C, EL), so it can stress-test SLW skin-effect claims. SW needs E=B=0 at the receiver. That needs a loop + E-probe + bifilar triad, a conductor that kills the first two, and a tone that remains on the third. This bench does not log that triad.

The distinction that matters

Hively’s EED promotes the Lorenz leftover to a physical field

$$ C = \nabla\cdot\mathbf{A} + \frac{1}{c^{2}}\frac{\partial\Phi}{\partial t}. $$

Both are claimed to avoid the classical skin effect (no circulating B, so no Faraday eddy currents). Only the SLW still has a longitudinal E that can drive conduction current in seawater or steel. Long-range hull-transparent signalling is a stronger theoretical SW candidate than SLW — the two must not be treated as the same measurement.

Joule loss: implication for SLW vs SW

In a linear conductor the local ohmic (Joule) power density is

$$ p_{\mathrm{Joule}} = \mathbf{J}\cdot\mathbf{E} = \sigma E^{2}. $$
  • SLW: nonzero $\mathbf{E}_{L}$ ⇒ expect ohmic attenuation in seawater or steel even if the classical eddy-current skin effect is suppressed (no circulating $\mathbf{B}$).
  • SW: $\mathbf{E}=0$ ⇒ no $\sigma E^{2}$ term ⇒ a theoretical hull / seawater transparency advantage relative to SLW.

The table row Ohmic / Joule loss in a conductor already encodes that split. Cage and seawater runs on this bench therefore stress-test SLW claims; they do not by themselves demonstrate SW transparency.

What this bench actually measures

The working apparatus is an SLW-class launcher/receiver:

  • balun monopole (1296 MHz build: close-fitting sleeve / bazooka; patent Fig. 2A skirt is historical) and/or bifilar pancake
  • TinySA / conventional RF chain
  • Faraday-cage and seawater-cell attenuation runs
  • PMT / optical side channel as a downstream effect sensor, not a first-principles C meter

Those antennas couple to the pair ($C$, $\mathbf{E}_{L}$). They can support SLW-class statements about gradient-driven channels, Faraday penetration vs TEM skin estimates, and seawater loss on a channel that still has $\mathbf{E}_{L}$. They cannot support “the surviving tone is a pure SW.” Cage penetration and PMT response are not SW proof.

Why the same bench is not an SW detector

  1. No E-null at the receiver. Bifilar or balun monopole still responds to residual longitudinal E.
  2. No independent field inventory. SW needs three simultaneous channels: loop → $\mathbf{B}$; short dipole / E-probe → $\mathbf{E}_{L}$; bifilar / second balun monopole → $C$ + residual $\mathbf{E}_{L}$. SW is only when the first two die in a conductor and the third remains. This bench does not log that triad.
  3. No field-free potential sensor (Josephson/SQUID or Puthoff-class $A$–$\Phi$ architecture) in the current RF chain. Naming an architecture is not evidence a test was run.
  4. No isolated information channel. Public EED work shows CW/swept tones through cages, not a modulated payload that exists only in $C$. Zimmerman plasma-tube work is a different ontology. Meyl kits are not used as evidence here.

Puthoff potential communication (NASA/CR-2005-213749 §3.4.2.2)

The MSE Technology Applications survey for NASA Langley, Advanced Energetics for Aeronautical Applications: Volume II (NASA/CR-2005-213749, Alexander, April 2005), §3.4.2.2, summarizes Harold E. Puthoff’s U.S. Patent US 5,845,220, titled Communication Method and Apparatus with Signals Comprising Scalar and Vector Potentials without Electromagnetic Fields.

  • Transmit framing (as reported): a time-varying signal of scalar and vector potentials without accompanying electromagnetic fields.
  • Receive framing (as reported): an electromagnetically shielded, cryogenic Josephson Junction converts the penetrating scalar/vector potentials into conventional EM that an ordinary radio can detect.
  • MSE reportage of Puthoff’s answers: only potential is transmitted and received; no energy is transferred as shielded $E$/$B$; and the system was built and tested “as part of a classified project” (quoted here only as NASA contractor survey reportage).

Mapping to this page: the patent’s “potentials without EM fields” framing sits closer to Hively’s SW ($E=B=0$, $C\neq 0$) than to SLW (which keeps $\mathbf{E}_{L}$ and therefore the $\sigma E^{2}$ Joule term). That is an ontology map, not a replication claim.

Not public SW evidence for this bench: the classified-project remark in the NASA survey is not treated here as a public, replicated, modulated SW-only link through seawater and steel. It does not move the Core lock-in or the Public summary. Do not cite it (or Meyl demos) as SW evidence on these pages.

What “fine for SLW” means

  • launcher family: gradient-driven, B suppressed
  • cage and seawater cells: right stress tests for no-classical-skin-effect claims
  • PMT / decay-rate side channels: optional SLW effects, not SW proof
  • label results SLW (or “SLW-like, $E_{L}$ not excluded”)

SLW still has $\mathbf{E}_{L}$, so $\sigma E^{2}$ loss in seawater is expected even if eddy-current skin effect is not.

What would have to be added for an SW claim

  1. Launch with the existing gradient-driven source.
  2. Receive on the three-channel set (loop / E-probe / bifilar).
  3. Insert conductivity (seawater cell, then steel plate/can).
  4. Require: loop and E-probe die; bifilar tone remains; TEM leakage paths closed.
  5. AM or slow FSK on the drive; recover the same spectrum on the surviving channel.

Repo stance: SLW-class hardware; SW requires $E=B=0$ isolation, a three-channel ($\mathbf{B}$ / $\mathbf{E}_{L}$ / $C$) conductor test, and a modulated payload on the surviving $C$ channel.
