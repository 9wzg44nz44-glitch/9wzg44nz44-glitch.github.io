Hively's Joule-loss term, quoted, and what it gives in seawater

HYP Hively & Loebl, “Classical and extended electrodynamics”, Phys. Essays 32, 112 (2019). PDF on this hub. The revised energy balance, Eq. (44), contains the Joule (Ohmic) term J·E:

∂/∂t ( B²/2μ + C²/2μ + εE² ) + ∇·( E×B/μ + CE/μ ) + J·E = ρC/(εμ) (Hively & Loebl 2019, Eq. 44, as printed) “Equation (44) has new energy density terms: scalar field energy (C²/2μ), SLW energy (CE/μ), and a power source (ρC/εμ).”

In their Appendix A they use Ohm's law, J = σE, “for linearly conductive media”. After Eq. (36) they write: “The resultant electric field is EL = JL/σ … Thus, no eddy currents occur, so the SLW is unimpeded by the skin effect.” They relate σ to the loss part of the permittivity: “σ = εoε″ω”. Their SLW impedance is Eq. (37):

Z = |E_r| / |C/μ_oμ'| = √(μ_o/ε_o) · √(μ'/ε') · [1 − 1/(jkr)] / [1 − j tan(δ_ε)], tan δ_ε = ε″/ε' (Eq. 37)

HYP Erratum, Phys. Essays 35, 320 (2022) (doi:10.4006/0836-1398-35.3.320). It corrects Appendix A and concludes: “Physically, Eq. (A6) means that the SLW is not subject to resistive loss in conductive media. The same no-loss result occurs from the energy balance equation, Eq. (44), by substituting for (ρ/ε) from Eq. (22) and by substituting for JL from Eq. (21) with B = 0; then cancelling terms. Consequently, the SLW is unconstrained by the skin effect and power loss in resistive media.” So Hively's own term, as the Erratum evaluates it, gives αSLW = 0. On this page that is a non-default toggle. The 2019 Appendix A reached the same no-loss conclusion by a different route (Eq. A7). Hively's patent (US 9,306,527) lists “E, C, and J exchange energy in conductive media” (Table 1, EQNS 11–17).

ASSUMPTION (ours) · page default Joule-literal reading (default, hub rule: include Hively's Joule-loss term for SLW in conductive media). Suppose the Joule heat J·E = σE² is not cancelled but drains the SLW power flow S = |CE/μ| = E²/|Z| (CE/μ sits inside the divergence in Eq. 44, so we treat it as the SLW power flow), using Eq. (37) to relate C to E. Then dS/dr = −σE² = −σ|Z| S, so the field attenuation is:

α_J = σ_H |Z| / 2, σ_H = ε_0 ε″ ω, |Z| = η_0 / (√ε' · |1 − j tan δ|) (far field, kr ≫ 1, μ' = 1)

This is our algebra built from Hively's Eqs. (37) and (44). It is not an equation printed by Hively, and the Erratum says the terms cancel instead. At 1057.8 MHz, 15 °C, salinity 35 (Meissner–Wentz): ε′ = {{ep}}, ε″ = {{epp}}, σH = {{sigH}}, |Z| = {{Z}}, so αJ = {{aJ}} = {{aJdB}}: 1/e length {{dJ}}.
