Experiment C · SNR companion v0 · illustrative · not a measurement

SLW POUT, Ohmic path loss, and atom / PMT SNR

Toy for sizing how much longitudinal SLW power leaves the transmitter, how geometric + Ohmic path loss eat it before a distant hydrogen (or clock) atom, and whether a dual-band PMT could recover an AM information envelope. Parent protocol: experiment-c.html. Contested physics — numbers are educational cartoons.

Do not forget Ohmic loss on SLW. B=0 ⇒ no eddy / classical skin pile-up, but E∥ ≠ 0 ⇒ J=σE and pJoule=σE∥². Pure SW (E=0) has no σE² term — toggle below. 8 GHz RF is still not a 10.2 eV photon; ηatom is a free coupling until measured.

Equations (as coded)

Hively / JPCS isotropic SLW cartoon (patent EQN. 15 · JPCS Eq. 22 shape):
  P_OUT = I_peak² · Z0 / (4π)          Z0 ≈ 376.73 Ω   [or drive from P_RF · η_launch]
  S_geom(r) = P_OUT / (4π r²)          free-space geometric spreading

Ohmic (SLW only — Hively EQNs 11–17 energy exchange; local Joule):
  p_Joule = σ E∥²
  transient patent form: E ∝ exp(−ε t / σ)     (EQN. 12 family)
  path toy:  Att_ohm_dB = 8.686 · α_ohm · L     (α_ohm = 0 for SW mode)
  S(r) = S_geom(r) · 10^(−Att_ohm_dB/10)

At the atom (placeholder until a cross-section exists):
  P_interact ≈ S(r) · A_atom · η_atom
  R_signal ≈ (P_interact / E_gap) · QE · T_optics · duty     E_gap = 10.2 eV
  SNR_counts ≈ R_signal / sqrt(R_signal + R_dark)   (Poisson, 1 s gate)

JPCS also writes SLW impedance with JL=σEL and tan δε=ε″/ε′ (Eq. 21). This page’s α_ohm slider is the experimenter-facing stand-in for that conductive-media Ohmic exchange until a medium-specific α(σ,ω) table is filled. Geometric 1/r² alone is not Ohmic.
