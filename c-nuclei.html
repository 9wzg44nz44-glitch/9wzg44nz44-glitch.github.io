C-field interaction with nuclei — a publishable model sketch

Educational model for dynamical C × nuclei in Hively extended electrodynamics (EED). It is a theory/experiment design note for a falsifiable coupling — not a claim that LENR, anomalous heat, decay-rate shifts, gravity modification, or mass change were measured on this bench.

Public summary (do not strengthen): This page proposes a falsifiable coupling sketch between Hively’s dynamical scalar field C and nuclear degrees of freedom. It is a theory/experiment design note. It does not claim anomalous heat, decay-rate shifts, or mass change were observed here.
Scope lock: This is not an SW detector (see SW vs SLW). PMT / decay-rate side channels remain optional SLW effects, not SW proof. Adjacent LENR / AHE / Alzofon–Sokol threads are labeled adjacent — not Hively C proof.

What Hively’s C is

From Hively & Land, J. Phys.: Conf. Ser. 1956 012011 (2021), and the hub’s SW vs SLW note: EED promotes the Lorenz leftover to a physical dynamical field

C ≡ ∇·A + (1/c²) ∂Φ/∂t

with wave equation

∇²C − (1/c²)∂²C/∂t² = −μ (∂ρ/∂t + ∇·J)

and energy density ∼ C²/(2μ). Split used on this hub:

  • SLW: B=0, C≠0, longitudinal EL ∥ ∇C
  • SW: E=B=0, C≠0

Hively’s short-time charge-fluctuation reading of the RHS (Heisenberg-style continuity mismatch) is his interpretation; it needs independent tests. Reed & Hively, Symmetry 12, 2110 (2020), is a related EED companion when a deeper math tip is useful — not required for this nuclear sketch.

Why nuclei (the publishable hook)

Nuclei carry charge multipoles, magnetic moments, and (for quadrupole nuclei) couple to electric field gradients (EFG). If C is a real scalar EM degree of freedom with energy density ∼ C²/(2μ), the open question is whether it couples to nuclear charge/current multipoles beyond ordinary E/B. That coupling — bounded, falsifiable, honestly framed — is what would make a paper. A null bound is publishable too.

Proposed interaction model proposal

Four channels. All are sketches; the microscopic operator for Channel A is not settled.

Channel A · charge / continuity

Back-action of C onto nuclear charge

EED’s source for C is (∂ρ/∂t + ∇·J). Nuclear transitions rearrange charge; a back-action of C onto nuclear charge density would appear as a phase or rate shift. Effective sketches (to be bounded experimentally):

δHC ∼ gC · Qnuc · C   or   ∫ ρnuc C dV

with gC a dimensionless coupling. For quadrupole nuclei, prefer

δH ∼ (κ/2) Qij ∂i∂j χC

where χC is a potential whose Laplacian relates to C. Keep honest: the microscopic operator is not settled — the experiment bounds effective couplings, it does not invent them.

Channel B · EFG bridge (Hubler) · not SLW hardware

PAC / EFG as a nuclear readout

Hubler (2023), J. Condensed Matter Nucl. Sci. 38, 78–88, uses perturbed angular correlation (PAC; 181Hf→181Ta) to measure EFG at the nucleus in PdH/D. That paper is AHE / phonon · LENR-adjacent — not a bifilar or Faraday-cage protocol (see Library). EFG is an ordinary electric-field-gradient observable at nuclear sites. An SLW-class longitudinal E (or a C-correlated gradient) that modulates the local EFG would be a legitimate, measurable nuclear readout — even if AHE/phonon claims stay adjacent and unlabeled as proven on this hub.

Channel C · spin / DNP · adjacent, not C proof

Ventura–Sokol / Falcon / Alzofon as a parallel program

Mainstream DNP/DNO (Jeffries, Dynamic Nuclear Orientation, 1963): microwave-driven transfer of electron polarization to nuclei near EPR — an accepted NMR-enhancement method. That is ordinary condensed-matter physics.

Falcon Space / Mark Sokol (APEC coverage by Tim Ventura; public Falcon materials) test Alzofon’s hypothesis that nuclear orientation might affect apparent mass / inertia. Framing on this hub:

  • Public sources only (e.g. Falcon DNO–mass PDF; Alzofon 1981 AIAA; Ventura/APEC interviews as journalism).
  • S/X-band DNP inside a Faraday cage is a nuclear-spin control channel — useful as “nuclei as sensors/actuators.”
  • Alzofon–Sokol inertia/gravity claims are speculative hypotheses, not accepted physics, and are not Hively C proof.

Mapping: DNP is a parallel “nuclei as sensors/actuators” program this hub refuses to exclude; it is not evidence that dynamical C couples to nuclei.

Channel D · decay rates · contested

Controlled side channel, not evidence in hand

Literature claims of environmental / solar modulation of decay rates (Fischbach, Jenkins, and others) exist. Metrology reviews (Pommé et al.; Norman/Cooper-style nulls) attribute many reported signals to systematics. Frame carefully: somewhat discussed / contested, not “well established.”

Proposal: decay-counting as a controlled side channel near an SLW-class source, with temperature / humidity / background locks — the same caution already on SW vs SLW for PMT / decay-rate side channels (optional SLW effects, not SW proof). This is not evidence already in hand on this bench.

Falsifiable experiment sketch

Educational design. Part 97 / licensed RF where applicable. Success = a published bound (or a clean null) on |δλ/λ| or EFG shift vs a C-proxy amplitude.

  • Primary launcher: SLW-class — 1296 MHz sleeve + bifilar or 433 MHz balls; keep the two feeds electrically separate (same rule as the lab pages).
  • Sensors (pick 2+): (i) Geiger/scintillator + known β/γ source with environmental logging; (ii) optional NMR/EPR or simple proton-precession if available; (iii) PAC/EFG only with collaboration access.
  • Controls: RF off / TEM stub / Faraday wrap / distance scan / temperature lock.
  • Explicit: this is not an SW detector (SW vs SLW). PMT / decay side channels are optional SLW effects, not SW proof.
