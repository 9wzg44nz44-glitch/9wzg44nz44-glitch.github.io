Machine-shop drawings — SMA bulkhead on Al hollow spheres

Printable sheets for mounting Amphenol 901-9875 (SMA-F bulkhead, 1/4-36 UNEF) on catalog 3003 aluminum hollow balls. Feed philosophy: coax into the interior; bulkhead body / outer conductor bonded to the shell as shield (Monstein–Wesley style). Educational; licensed amateur RF only.

Hole facts: Exp A KB-1820 (¾″) ships with no hole — shop drills from solid. Exp B KB-2016 (2.50″) ships with a pre-drilled ¼″ hole — do not tap that hole as a 1/4-36 tap drill. Preferred clearance for both: 6.5 mm (≈ 0.256″).

Preferred path (A)

Exterior flange/boss + Ø6.5 mm clearance through flange and wall + conductive bond of 901-9875 body to shell. Avoid relying on an interior locknut (¾″ ID is too tight).

MW manufacturability note

Monstein & Wesley Fig. 1 used a machined ball with an 18 mm shaft and 34 mm base (thick feed). Our flange/boss is the catalog-ball analogue: thicken the feed region so the SMA can be mounted without tapping a thin stamped wall. Not a full Fig. 1 remachine.

Material for the experiment: Support and rotator must be non-metallic in the RF path (no steel fasteners next to the rods if you can help it — nylon/plastic). Preferred: HDPE sheet or acrylic plate. Acceptable: dry plywood / MDF with plastic bushings. Styrofoam (EPS/XPS): electrically usually fine at 433 MHz (low εᵣ), but too soft for a logged 360° rotator — use only for a static dry-fit mock-up. Hub prior note: HDPE/acrylic for the non-metallic rotator (monstein / setup shop list).

Preferred materials

  • HDPE sheet (best all-around: tough, low RF fuss, easy drill)
  • Acrylic / polycarbonate plate (stiffer; watch cracking when drilling)
  • Dry plywood + nylon bushings (acceptable if dry and logged)
  • Styrofoam: mock-up / alignment only — not for the logged rotator

Manual vs motorized

Start manual: lazy-Susan + degree ring is enough for brass baseline T(φ). Motorize later if you want denser angle steps or hands-off sweeps — stepper under the base with angle readout; keep motors/metal below the rod plane. Not required for a valid first article.

Work order (support + jig)

WORK ORDER — Exp B polarizer support + rotator
Material: HDPE or acrylic plate, ≈ 380 × 380 × 50–75 mm (15″ × 15″ × 2–3″ = 1 ft 3 in square × 2–3 in thk) (starting).
1. Layout 3×3 grid: pitch 173 mm (6.81″ / 6-13/16″); outer centers 346 mm (13.62″ / 13-5/8″) apart; center hole on block center.
2. Drill 9 × Ø≈3.2 mm (0.126″) thru (fit 1/8″ rods snug). Deburr holes. Dry-fit all 9 rods parallel.
3. Mount block on non-metallic (or low-profile) turntable / lazy-Susan.
4. Affix 0–360° scale on fixed base; pointer on rotating block. Define φ=0 = rods ∥ TX–RX.
5. Optional motor: couple stepper under base; verify angle vs scale; park motor mass below rods.
6. Log material, pitch, thk, manual vs motor in CSV with T(φ).
