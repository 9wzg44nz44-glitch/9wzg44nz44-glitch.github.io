Experiment A — 1296 MHz Faraday-cage

Sleeve-balun stub + bifilar pancake, two RadioScreen mesh tents. Licensed 23 cm. This is the Hively-style cage replication at 1296.000 MHz (λ = 231.3 mm). Electrically separate from Experiment B: do not mix the 433 MHz ball feed into this coax.

Sleeve, not skirt, for the build. US 9,306,527 Fig. 2A is an annular skirt balun: larger diameter than the coax outer, extending backward from the feed, outer conductor tied to the top of the skirt. The patent says that λ/4 path cancels return current on the outside of the tubular second conductor and also acts as a small ground-plane/counterpoise (currents on the center conductor and the outside of the skirt both +Z). That fat skirt can launch extra TEM. For this 1.3 GHz replication, build a close-fitting sleeve (bazooka choke), not that skirt — paraphrase of Hively 2026 personal communication; no private email is reproduced. Same choke physics (λ/4 shorted line → high Z for common-mode) without a large-diameter radiating skirt. No 3λ ground disk.

First-article numbers (1296.000 MHz)

λ = 231.3 mm. λ/4 sleeve = 57.8 mm in air (trim if the sleeve–jacket gap has VF < 1). First-article stub = λ/10 = 23.1 mm of center conductor (λ/4 = 57.8 mm remains the TEM-style control stub). RG-405/U or SMA pigtail. Brass/copper tube snug over the jacket, soldered to the shield at the cold end (away from the stub tip), open at the feed/tip. 23 cm BPF still in the TX chain inside the Faraday wrap (setup.html#bpf).

Experiment B — 433.59 MHz balls + polarizer rods

Monstein and Wesley 2002 geometry: aluminium ball antennas, brass (then optional hysteresis) λ/2 rods as a polarizer. No balun of any type — not a sleeve, not a skirt, not a bazooka. Coax into the ball, outer conductor as shield (MW Fig. 1 / Amphenol 901-9875). Do not put Experiment A sleeve-build steps on this bench.

No balun. The 433.59 MHz sphere feed is MW “centrally fed, outer as shield.” Experiment A’s λ/4 sleeve lives on RG-405 at 1296 MHz, not on this ball. Keep the RF feeds electrically separate even if both antennas later ride in one Pelican as cargo.

What this bench is

TX ball → optional 3×3 rod cube at angle φ → RX ball. Polarizer test for the direction of E, not a Faraday-cage skin-depth test and not a bifilar pancake. 70 cm Technician-legal with a license. Educational; longitudinal E is not settled physics.

Shared field kit — one TX Pelican, one RX case

Planned end-state for taking both experiments into the field. Not a claim the cube has been built, ballasted, or dunked. Plastic Pelican = hydrostatic shell; RadioScreen mesh = Faraday. Sequential is the default. Full write-up with SKUs and anchors: kit.html.

In one sentence

One cubical watertight TX case (1296 sleeve-stub sled and 433 TX sphere in separate foam wells, whole inner pack in RadioScreen). Second case for RX, usually lid open, mesh tent over the instruments so two TinySA screens stay readable. Not submerged. Pelican 1200 remains the small coil/TinySA travel case on setup.html#shop — it is not this hull.
