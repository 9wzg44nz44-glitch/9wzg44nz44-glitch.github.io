Experiment A · 1296.000 MHz · 23 cm

Faraday-cage: sleeve-balun stub + bifilar pancake

Two RadioScreen mesh tents. TX tent: licensed 23 cm source, 23 cm BPF still in the TX chain inside the Faraday wrap (setup.html#bpf), RG-405/U or SMA pigtail with a sleeve (bazooka) balun and a short center-conductor stub. RX tent: fold-in-half bifilar pancake and TinySA Ultra 406. No coax between tents on a sealed run.

Build this

Sleeve balun, not a skirt

Close-fitting λ/4 tube over the jacket, soldered to the shield at the end away from the stub tip, open at the feed. Same choke (λ/4 shorted line → high-Z common-mode) without a large radiating skirt. How-to + side view: setup.html#sleeve.

1296.000 MHz

First-article lengths

λ = 231.3 mm. λ/4 sleeve = 57.8 mm in air (trim if VF < 1). First-article stub λ/10 = 23.1 mm of center conductor. λ/4 = 57.8 mm is a TEM-control stub, not the first article. Brass/copper tube snug on the jacket. No 3λ ground disk.

Patent history

Fig. 2A is a skirt

US 9,306,527 Fig. 2A is an annular skirt: larger than the coax, extends backward, outer tied to the top of the skirt; λ/4 cancel + counterpoise. A fat skirt can launch extra TEM. Hively 2026 pers. comm. (paraphrase, no email): for 1.3 GHz, build a sleeve balun, not a skirt.

Experiment B · 433.59 MHz · 70 cm

Balls + polarizer rods — no balun

Monstein & Wesley 2002: identical aluminium ball antennas, coax fed to the inside of the sphere with the outer conductor as shield (Fig. 1). Nine λ/2 brass rods as a rotatable polarizer-analyzer. Optional unpublished Hively hysteresis-rod swap uses the same cube geometry.

No balun on B. Do not add a sleeve or a skirt to the ball feed. Coax into the ball, outer as shield — MW Fig. 1 / Amphenol 901-9875 already purchased. Sleeve-balun steps live only on Experiment A (setup.html#sleeve).

What B is

433.59 MHz (λ ≈ 69.1 cm; λ/2 rods = 34.6 cm). Technician-legal 70 cm in the US with a valid license. Polarizer test for the direction of E, not a Faraday-cage skin-depth test and not a bifilar pancake.

Feed

Coax into the ball; outer as shield. Catalog path: 2.50″ 3003 Aluminum hollow + SMA bulkhead through the existing ¼″ hole. No sleeve steps on this bench.

Shared field kit · end-state, not built

One TX cube, one RX cube, sequential default

The kit carries both antennas as cargo — 1296 sleeve-stub sled and 433 TX sphere in the TX case; two TinySA screens in the RX case. It does not mix RF feeds. Pancake protocol stays on setup.html. 433 parts stay on monstein.html / sphere.html. Full write-up: kit.html.

TX cube

One cubical watertight Pelican. Foam holds the 1296 sleeve-stub sled and the 433 TX sphere. Inner pack wrapped in RadioScreen. Plastic hull = hydrostatic shell; mesh = Faraday. Flat sides for aiming the stub axis.

RX cube

Second case, usually lid open, mesh tent so two TinySA screens stay readable (1296 and 433). Not submerged.

Sequential first

Default is sequential. Simultaneous only if no interference and a 4th TinySA Ultra is bought (three on hand). Sequential TX+RX = 2 instruments; simultaneous dedicated = 4. With 3, one TX is switched/shared.

  • Harmonics — 1296/3 = 432 MHz, 1.59 MHz from 433.59; 433.59×3 = 1300.77 vs 1296. Simultaneous needs 23 cm BPF on 1296 TX and 70 cm BPF on 433 TX, separate foam wells, both antennas inside mesh.
  • Ballast — 0340 buoyancy 220 lb: empty cube floats. Do not drill the hull. NRS 1″ HD cam straps through the four fold-down handles + shot bags. No VHB pad-eyes as the primary seawater path.
  • Depth — IP67 = 1 m / 30 min, not continuous seawater. First: pool / shallow FL. Later seawater is a logged separate step.
  • Cases — First buy: Pelican 0340 (18×18×18 in interior). Pelican 1200 is not the hull — it stays the small TinySA/coil travel case on setup.html#shop.

Not first buy (one-liners): Nanuk 960 ~$375 rectangle; SKB 3i-2217-12; B&W Type 5500 cheaper near-cube; Zarges K470 aluminum IP54 on US 40564 / IP67 EU 380366 — metal cages RF unless antennas sit outside on glands; Blue Robotics WTE is a cylinder; Hammond 1554 / Hoffman Al too small or not 6P. Metal Faraday+hull later (corrosion, no see-through; Slayson/Blue Robotics small/expensive). First buy: plastic cube + internal mesh.
