Scalar longitudinal waves — one desk, many notebooks

This site unifies Dan Britton’s GitHub Pages on Nikolova–Zimmerman vector-potential experiments, Hively Extended Electrodynamics (SLW), and Meyl’s potential-vortex reading of Maxwell. It is a study aid for patents, papers, and a 1.3 GHz Faraday-cage lab — not a claim that SLW is established physics.

Who this is for

Licensed amateur-radio experimenters, students of classical electrodynamics who want a map of the “missing-term” literature, and anyone trying to read US 8,165,531 and US 9,306,527 without drowning in twenty-six separate GitHub Pages. It is not for medical devices, “free energy,” or unlicensed transmitters.

Nikolova–Zimmerman

Longitudinal A, plasma detector

McMaster CEM-R-46 (2007), Zimmerman QEX Jul/Aug 2011, US 8,165,531 B2. 1.296 GHz TM01 waveguide launch; U-shaped fluorescent tube as a differential Aharonov–Bohm detector. Claimed ~1.5 km link.

Hively EED

SLW, bifilar, Faraday copper

US 9,306,527 B1 (2016), Physics Essays 2019, IARD 2020. Irrotational current, dynamical scalar C, B≈0, skirt-balun monopole and bifilar pancake. Original tests near 8 GHz; this hub scales the replication to 1.3 GHz.

Meyl

Potential vortices

Extended Faraday law with τ2. Mapped here as a third vocabulary, not as a foundation for the lab. Over-unity and superluminal claims are recorded as claims and are not used in any energy budget.

How the hub is organized

Learn

Side-by-side physics, hardware, citations with Google Patents links, Zimmerman’s 1296 MHz stovepipe TM01 transmitter, and how the two detectors are supposed to work.

Compare

Comparison table plus the briefing’s interactive SNR toy — clearly labeled illustrative — with the paper/patent formulas shown beside it.

Tools

Hively-style bifilar pancake calculator defaulted to 1.300 GHz (presets 1296 MHz and 1.30 GHz), RG-405 stub + skirt balun, AWG table, winding polarity.

Lab

1.3 GHz Faraday-cage replication of Hively US 9,306,527. HAM legal box, copper skin-depth table, BOM, controls, CSV log, publishing checklist, safety. Licensed 23 cm source. No magnetron.

Setup

Bench walk-through: Cricut-style sticky “gummy” mat so 22/24 AWG doesn’t tangle, series-opposing pancake, then stub, skirt, cage, and first measurements. Open the setup page.
