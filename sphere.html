Is a catalog 2.50″ hollow 3003 sphere an acceptable Fig. 1 substitute?

This page is a correspondence aid for Dan Britton to send to L.M. Hively. It asks whether a procurable hollow aluminium ball plus an SMA bulkhead can stand in for the machined 6 cm ball antennas in Monstein & Wesley, Europhys. Lett. 59, 514 (2002), Fig. 1. The question, stated plainly

Is this hollow 2.50″ 3003 aluminium sphere (K-Mac KB-2016, existing ¼″ hole) plus an Amphenol 901-9875 SMA-F bulkhead through that hole an acceptable substitute for Fig. 1’s machined 60 mm aluminium ball with a 10H7/3H7 coaxial cavity, or does the missing internal shaft/bore / the 3.35 mm wall / the 3003 alloy change the feed (outer as shield vs center conductor) enough that you would not treat them as equivalent for a 433.59 MHz ball-antenna polarizer bench?

No private email is reproduced here. Hively is not a GitHub collaborator on this repo. This page is an educational comparison for that correspondence, not a claim that SLW is settled physics and not a coauthored paper.

What Fig. 1 actually shows

Monstein & Wesley used identical aluminium ball antennas, 6 cm diameter, coax fed to the inside of the sphere with the outer conductor as shield. Fig. 1 of the 2002 letter is a machine-shop sketch (dimensions in millimetres):

  • R30 — 60 mm outside diameter (radius 30 mm).
  • 10H7 / 3H7 coax bore (outer / inner fits on the coaxial cavity).
  • 18 mm shaft into the ball.
  • 34 mm base.
  • Centrally fed; outer of the ball as shield.

We do not host the IOP/EPLA PDF. Author-hosted copy (filename typo “electroydnamic”): jamespaulwesley.org. Full Experiment B briefing: monstein.html.

Proposed procurable substitute

Instead of machining Fig. 1 from solid aluminium, the catalog path is a hollow 3003 ball that already has a ¼″ hole, plus a stock SMA bulkhead through that hole.

K-Mac 2.50″ hollow 3003

  • 2.50″ (63.5 mm) OD — 3.5 mm larger than Fig. 1’s 60.0 mm.
  • 3003 aluminium, hollow, wall 0.132″ (3.35 mm).
  • Existing ¼″ hole — intended for the SMA bulkhead, not a 10H7/3H7 internal coax cavity.
  • Need two (TX + RX). Same vendor also lists 2.00″ KB-2014 and 3.00″ KB-2015; Butterworth’s reconstruction used ~3″ Al.

Amphenol 901-9875 SMA-F bulkhead

As purchased: two 901-9875 bulkheads (invoice 2 Sep 2026). Photo of the part in-hand will be added when Dan sends it.

Gender chain on this bench: TinySA Ultra RF port is SMA-F (hole). Jumper is SMA-M/M (pin both ends). Bulkhead through the ¼″ hole is SMA-F on the outside so the cable pin screws on. Prefer 901-9875 as the “coax into the interior” part (crimp RG-316, center soldered inside the sphere). See monstein.html#bulkhead.

Still electrically small

At 433.59 MHz, λ = c/f ≈ 0.692 m. The MW ball has a/λ ≈ 0.043; the 2.50″ ball has a/λ ≈ 0.046. Both are ka ≪ 1 (k = 2π/λ). The open question is not “is it a 6 cm dipole at UHF?” — it is whether dropping the internal coaxial shaft/bore, using a thin 3003 wall, and feeding through a stock SMA bulkhead still counts as Fig. 1’s “centrally fed, outer as shield” geometry.

What this page is not

  • Not a Meyl kit. Meyl Demo / Experimental kits are HF Tesla pancakes with spherical electrodes (kHz–low MHz). Do not treat them as a 433 MHz ball feed. See monstein.html#meyl-kit.
  • Sleeve balun, not skirt on any follow-on monopole work Hively suggested for this same Experiment B geometry.
